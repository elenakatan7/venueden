Our Story

We built the
partner we
always wanted.

Every pop-up we’d ever been close to had the same problem. Finding the space was hard. Then the lease, the permits, the build, the signage, the staff — each with its own vendor, its own timeline, and nobody holding the whole thing.

Venue Den is the company we kept wishing we could hire: one small team that finds the space, runs the build, and answers the phone at every step in between. Elena leads the client side, Eric runs venues and leasing, Ryan owns the build. You’ll know all three by name.
