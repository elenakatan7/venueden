About

The crew behind
the pop-up.

Great storefronts don’t ask for attention. They earn curiosity. That’s the idea behind everything we build.

Why pop-ups. A store you can walk into does what no feed can: it puts the product in someone’s hands.

Why New York. Nowhere rewards a great storefront like this city. The whole world walks past your window.

Why VenueDen. A pop-up used to mean a dozen vendors and nobody holding the whole thing. We’re one team that finds the space, runs the build, and answers the phone. Pop-up retail, nothing else.
