Pop-up space in Nolita.

Intimate blocks, loyal locals, jewel-box storefronts. What a Nolita space costs, who it's right for, and how we produce a pop-up there end to end.

Why Nolita

Nolita is SoHo's quieter neighbor and, for the right brand, the better call. Elizabeth, Mott, and Mulberry are lined with small, characterful storefronts and shoppers who come specifically to discover something new. The scale is intimate by design — a jewel-box space here feels curated, not cramped — and the neighborhood's boutique culture means a pop-up reads as a find rather than an ad. You're one block from SoHo's foot traffic at a meaningfully lower cost.

What a Nolita pop-up costs

Nolita generally costs less than prime SoHo while keeping the downtown address. The variables:

  • The block. Elizabeth Street is the heart of it; Mott and Mulberry follow close behind.
  • Size and run length. Most Nolita spaces are compact — which keeps both rent and build-out budgets tighter than a big-floor launch.
  • Everything beyond rent. Build-out, insurance, permits, and staffing — smaller footprints keep these manageable.

We quote each space individually. See the full pop-up cost breakdown, then tell us what you're planning for a real number within one business day.

Who Nolita is right for

  • Emerging fashion labels that want discovery over scale.
  • Jewelry, accessories, and fragrance in considered, intimate formats.
  • Premium brands testing New York with a smaller first footprint.

If you need maximum reach and flagship energy, SoHo is one block over; if your audience is younger and Brooklyn-first, look at Williamsburg. We'll tell you honestly which block fits.

How we produce a pop-up in Nolita

We scout storefronts across Nolita — not from a fixed portfolio — and match the brand to the exact block: the neighbors, the walk-by rhythm, how the street feels on a Saturday. Then we handle the short-term lease, insurance and permits, design and build, fixtures and signage, staffing, and a clean strike at the end. Everything we handle, one team, one point of contact.
