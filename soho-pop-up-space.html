Pop-up space in SoHo.

The most-shopped blocks in New York. What a SoHo storefront costs, who it's right for, and how we produce a pop-up there end to end.

Why SoHo

SoHo is the highest-traffic retail neighborhood in New York and, for many brands, the whole reason to do a pop-up in the first place. Cast-iron facades, cobblestone blocks, and a constant flow of shoppers and tourists make it the city's flagship district — the place where an established brand makes a statement and gets seen by the most people in the least time. Broadway, Prince, Spring, Greene, and West Broadway are the arteries; the closer to them you are, the more the block costs.

What a SoHo pop-up costs

SoHo carries the premium rents in the city for short-term retail. Three things set the number:

  • The exact block. Prime Broadway and West Broadway frontage is the top of the market; side streets and upper floors cost less for similar footage.
  • Size and run length. A compact ground-floor space for a two-week drop prices very differently from a large floor held for a season.
  • Everything beyond rent. The build-out, insurance, permits, staffing, and security all scale with a high-visibility SoHo launch.

Because the range is wide, we quote every SoHo space individually. See how a full pop-up budget comes together in the cost breakdown, then tell us what you're planning for a real number within one business day.

Who SoHo is right for

SoHo rewards scale and polish. It's the right block for:

  • Established fashion and lifestyle brands making a flagship-level statement.
  • Beauty and footwear launches that want maximum reach and a photogenic build.
  • Well-funded DTC brands ready for a high-visibility physical moment.

If your launch is smaller, more intimate, or discovery-led, another neighborhood — Nolita, the Lower East Side, or Williamsburg — may serve you better and cost less. We'll tell you honestly which block fits the brand.

How we produce a pop-up in SoHo

We scout storefronts across SoHo — not from a fixed portfolio — and match the brand to the exact block: foot traffic, neighbors, and how the street feels at 6pm. Then we handle the short-term lease, insurance and permits, design and build, fixtures and signage, staffing, security, and a clean strike at the end. Everything we handle, one team, one point of contact.
