# Kith + Moncler Aesthetic Research

## Kith.com
- **Color**: Pure black background (#000000), pure white text — maximum contrast
- **Typography**: All-caps condensed sans-serif for navigation and labels; clean geometric sans for body
- **Layout**: Full-bleed imagery edge-to-edge, no padding on hero images; content stacked in dense editorial grids
- **Navigation**: Minimal top bar, black background, white text, very small type
- **Hero**: Video or full-screen image, no overlay text except minimal label
- **Grid**: Tight 2-col or 3-col product grids with no border-radius on images
- **Whitespace**: Minimal — images fill the space; text is sparse
- **Brand energy**: Streetwear luxury, confident, no-frills, raw

## Moncler.com
- **Color**: White/off-white background, black text — clean luxury
- **Typography**: Elegant serif for headlines ("The Language of Detail"), clean sans for body
- **Layout**: Full-bleed hero video/image, then editorial split layouts (image left, text right)
- **Navigation**: White background, black text, very thin/light weight
- **Hero**: Full-viewport video with minimal overlay text, centered or bottom-left
- **Grid**: Large editorial image tiles, generous spacing between sections
- **Whitespace**: Very generous — sections breathe
- **Brand energy**: European luxury, heritage, refined, cinematic

## Synthesis for VENUE Redesign

### What to take from KITH:
- Pure black (#000) as dominant background color
- All-caps tight tracking for section labels and nav
- Full-bleed imagery with zero border radius
- Dense, editorial grid layouts
- Maximum contrast — white on black
- Minimal UI chrome — let content breathe

### What to take from MONCLER:
- Cinematic full-viewport hero with video-like quality
- Elegant serif headlines with editorial weight
- Split-screen editorial layouts (image | text)
- Generous section spacing
- Refined, sparse copy — every word earns its place
- White/light sections as contrast breaks between dark sections

### New VENUE Design System:
- **Primary Background**: Pure black `#000000` / `oklch(0.05 0 0)`
- **Secondary Background**: Near-black `#0A0A0A` / `oklch(0.08 0 0)`
- **Light Section Background**: Warm white `#F5F3F0` / `oklch(0.96 0.005 80)`
- **Primary Text**: Pure white `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary Text**: Mid-gray `#888888`
- **Accent**: Keep clay/terracotta `oklch(0.58 0.11 44)` — now pops against black
- **Typography**: 
  - Display: Cormorant Garamond (elegant serif, like Moncler)
  - UI/Labels: DM Mono or tight tracking DM Sans uppercase (like Kith)
- **Images**: Full-bleed, zero border-radius, edge-to-edge
- **Borders**: Thin 1px lines in dark gray `oklch(0.18 0 0)`
- **Hover**: Subtle opacity shifts, no color changes
