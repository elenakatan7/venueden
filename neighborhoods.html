Why SoHo?

The world's most
imitated retail district.

SoHo is where brands prove themselves. The neighborhood's mix of international tourists, fashion-forward locals, and industry insiders creates a uniquely high-intent shopper — someone who doesn't stumble in, but seeks you out.

Cast-iron storefronts on Spring, Prince, and Mercer have hosted some of the most legendary retail activations in fashion history. A SoHo popup signals seriousness to the industry.
