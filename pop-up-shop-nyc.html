The guide

Opening a pop-up shop
in New York City.

What a pop-up space really costs, how fast you can launch, where it should open, and what it takes to produce — from the team that does this for a living.

What is a pop-up shop?

A pop-up shop is a short-term retail store — days, weeks, or a few months — opened to launch a product, test a neighborhood, run a seasonal moment, or give an online brand its first physical home. In New York, pop-up retail ranges from a capsule drop in a SoHo storefront to a full experiential build with fabrication, custom lighting, and staffed opening nights.

How much does a pop-up shop cost in NYC?

Most pop-up projects start around $12K and range significantly with five drivers:

  • The space — neighborhood, size, and length of the run drive rent more than anything else.
  • The build-out — paint and merchandising is one budget; custom millwork, lighting, and fabrication is another.
  • The timeline — compressed launches cost more in expedited trades and freight.
  • Staffing — floor staff, security, and cleaning scale with hours and foot traffic.
  • Production needs — signage, furniture rental, AV, photography, and event nights.

Every project is quoted individually — tell us what you’re planning and we’ll give you an honest number within one business day.

How fast can a pop-up launch?

A typical timeline is four to eight weeks from brief to open: one to three weeks to source and sign the right space, then two to five weeks of design, build, and merchandising depending on the scope. Tight timelines are workable when the brief is clear.

Where should a pop-up open in NYC?

The neighborhood is half of why a pop-up works. The short version:

  • SoHo — flagship energy and global foot traffic; where established brands make statements.
  • Nolita — intimate blocks and loyal locals; ideal for emerging labels and jewel-box formats.
  • Tribeca — quiet luxury at a collector’s pace; showrooms and high-consideration product.
  • Williamsburg — young, early, first-to-know; the right first store for DTC brands.
  • Lower East Side & beyond — culture-led launches, galleries, and music moments.

We scout storefronts anywhere in New York — not from a fixed portfolio — and match the brand to the block: foot traffic, neighbors, and how the street feels at 6pm.

What does it take to produce a pop-up?

More than most brands expect — which is exactly why we exist. A short-term retail lease with insurance and permits. Design, construction, and fabrication. Lighting, fixtures, furniture, signage. Freight, receiving, merchandising. Staff, security, cleaning. And a proper strike that returns the keys broom-clean. See everything we handle — one team, one point of contact.
