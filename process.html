01
Discovery
The Concierge Call
We start with a 30-minute call to understand your brand, campaign goals, target audience, timeline, and budget. No forms. No RFPs. A real conversation with a real expert.
Same day
  • Brand & campaign brief
  • Budget expectations set
  • Timeline confirmed
  • Neighborhood shortlist identified
02
Space
Space Selection & Tour
Within 24 hours we'll share a curated shortlist of 3–5 spaces matched to your brief. We schedule tours, walk you through each space, and provide a side-by-side comparison including foot traffic data, nearby brand context, and build potential.
1–3 days
  • Curated space shortlist delivered
  • In-person tours arranged
  • Comparative analysis provided
  • Availability confirmed & held
03
Design
Concept & Creative Direction
Once a space is selected, our creative team develops a full concept: moodboard, floor plan, material palette, lighting scheme, and visual merchandising direction. You'll see exactly what your space will look and feel like before a single nail is hammered.
3–5 days
  • Moodboard & concept deck
  • Floor plan & traffic flow
  • Material & finish selections
  • 3D renderings (Flagship/Icon)
  • Permits filed
04
Build
Construction & Fabrication
Our crew takes over the space. Custom fixtures are fabricated off-site and installed with precision. We handle all electrical, lighting, AV, and finishing — and send daily photo updates so you're never in the dark. Move-in happens on a schedule you can set your calendar to.
5–14 days
  • Fixture fabrication
  • Structural modifications
  • Lighting & electrical install
  • Furniture delivery & placement
  • Signage installation
  • Pre-opening inspection
05
Launch
Open Doors
Launch day is the culmination — and it should feel that way. We handle the opening event, staff briefing, POS setup, and content capture. Your concierge is on-site for the first day to ensure everything runs exactly as planned.
Activation period
  • Staff briefing & training
  • POS & payment systems live
  • Launch event production
  • Photography & content capture
  • Daily operations support
  • Security & cleaning
06
After
Breakdown & Results
When the activation closes, we handle everything: strike, restoration, inventory pack-out, and return logistics. Two weeks later, you receive a full performance report: foot traffic, conversion, media value, social reach, and key learnings — plus recommendations for your next campaign.
1–2 weeks post-close
  • Space strike & restoration
  • Inventory logistics
  • Performance analytics report
  • Media coverage summary
  • Post-activation debrief call
