VenueDen is New York City's full-service pop-up platform. We connect brands with curated retail spaces and handle everything needed to launch an unforgettable activation — from lease negotiation and store design to staffing, production, and content. Unlike simple listing platforms, we're a true end-to-end partner.
Other platforms are directories. VenueDen is a production partner. We don't just connect you to a landlord and wish you luck — we handle design, build, staffing, logistics, content, and PR as a unified team. Every project gets a dedicated concierge who owns the entire experience from first call to closing night.
Our fastest turnaround is 5 business days from signed lease to open doors — for a pre-built, minimal activation in an existing space. A fully custom build typically takes 2–4 weeks from concept sign-off. We recommend reaching out 4–6 weeks before your target launch date, though we've handled last-minute campaigns with 72-hour notice before.
Fashion, beauty, streetwear, jewelry, technology, food & beverage, art, and lifestyle brands — DTC labels launching their first physical retail moment to established luxury houses testing new markets. We've worked with brands at every stage, from emerging designers with $8K budgets to global labels with $200K activations.
Our primary focus is Manhattan (SoHo, Nolita, NoHo, Tribeca, Chelsea, Flatiron, Meatpacking, Upper East Side) and Brooklyn (Williamsburg). We're continuously adding new neighborhoods and can source spaces in any NYC borough on request.
Most spaces have a 7-day minimum, though some listings allow weekend bookings (2–3 days). For NYFW, holiday, and other peak periods, minimums may increase to 2 weeks. If you need a shorter activation, our concierge team can identify spaces with flexible terms.

Most retail activations require a NYC Department of Consumer Affairs license if goods are being sold. Significant construction work requires DOB permits. If you're hosting events, a temporary event permit may be required. We handle all permit filing as part of our service — you'll never need to deal with the city directly.
Our insurance coverage and the lease agreement clearly define liability at each stage. Space damage during the rental period falls under your liability insurance (which we provide). Production-related damage during our build or strike is covered by our contractor's insurance. We document the space condition at move-in and move-out to protect all parties.
