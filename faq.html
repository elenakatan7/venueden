FAQ

Questions brands
and landlords ask us.

Do you source pop-up spaces?

Yes — it’s the heart of what we do. We search across New York for each brief: our own managed venues, our landlord network, or a direct negotiation with a property we’ve scouted for you.

Do you only work in New York?

New York City is our home and our specialty — SoHo, Nolita, Tribeca, Flatiron, the Lower East Side, Williamsburg, and beyond. If your launch is here, we know the streets, the landlords, and the fine print.

Do you work with landlords?

Closely. We activate vacant storefronts with professionally managed pop-ups: flexible terms, curated brands, and a space returned broom-clean. If you own retail space, see our landlords page.

Can you manage the whole production?

Yes. Design, build-out, fabrication, construction coordination, permits, signage and install, furniture and fixtures, security and staffing coordination, logistics, and breakdown — one team, one point of contact.

Do you handle construction and fabrication?

We coordinate trusted contractors, fabricators, and millworkers we’ve worked with across many builds — and we manage them daily: schedule, site quality, and budget.

Can you coordinate photo and video?

Yes, as optional production support. When you want the build and the finished space documented professionally, we bring in photographers and videographers we trust. We don’t do PR, press, or influencer campaigns.

How much does a pop-up cost?

Most pop-up projects start around $12K and range significantly with the space, build-out, timeline, staffing, and production needs. Every project gets a custom proposal — tell us what you’re planning and we’ll give you an honest number.

How quickly can a pop-up launch?

A typical timeline is four to eight weeks from brief to open, depending on the space and the build. Tight timelines are workable when the brief is clear — ask us.

Do you offer short-term leases?

Yes. We negotiate short-term retail agreements — days, weeks, or months — and handle terms, insurance requirements, and permits alongside the lease.

What kinds of brands do you work with?

Fashion, beauty, luxury, lifestyle, entertainment, and food & beverage — from emerging DTC labels opening their first store to global names testing a New York moment. See industries.
