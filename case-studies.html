The Brief

SKIMS needed a physical presence in New York for Valentine's Day 2024 — a moment to bring their digital community into a tactile brand world for the first time. The challenge: build a fully immersive environment in 10 days, in one of the most competitive blocks in Manhattan, without disrupting the exclusivity the brand had carefully cultivated online.

Our concierge team sourced the Spring Street Loft within 48 hours of the initial call. The creative direction was signed off on Day 3. Build began on Day 5. Doors opened on Valentine's Day.

What We Built

Space
1,800 sq ft Spring Street ground floor. Full custom build: pink travertine, steel fixtures, custom pink neon.
Events
Opening night influencer dinner for 40. Press preview on Day 2. Public opening Day 3.
Content
Full photography suite, brand video, and 60+ UGC moments captured and seeded.
Staffing
6 brand ambassadors, 2 security, 1 on-site concierge (VenueDen), daily cleaning.

"VenueDen made what felt impossible feel inevitable. We went from idea to open doors in 10 days, and the result looked like we'd been planning it for six months."

— Director of Brand Experience, SKIMS
