About This Space

Spring Street Loft is one of SoHo's most sought-after popup destinations — a raw yet refined ground-floor retail space with 38 feet of north-facing storefront windows and original cast-iron columns. The space has hosted activations for LVMH brands, emerging luxury labels, and DTC companies making their first foray into physical retail.

The open-plan layout accommodates everything from intimate brand presentations to fully built-out flagship experiences. A dedicated loading bay on Mercer Street provides seamless move-in, and the built-in lighting track can be fully reconfigured for any aesthetic.
