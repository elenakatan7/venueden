All questions
How much does a SoHo pop-up cost?

Most SoHo activations land between $40K and $250K all-in, depending on duration, build complexity, and space. Daily space rates start around $650. Use our cost estimator for a number in 60 seconds.

How fast can we open?

Our fastest concept-to-open was 21 days. A typical timeline is 6–8 weeks: one week for space, two to three for design and permits, the rest for build and merchandising.

Do you handle permits and insurance?

Yes — every permit, filing, and certificate between signing and opening day is on us. You never speak to the Department of Buildings.

Can you staff and run the store for us?

Fully. Trained brand-matched staff, managed inventory, daily reporting. Many clients never fly in until opening night.

What if we already have a space?

Every service works as a standalone module — design-and-build only, staffing only, or anything in between.
