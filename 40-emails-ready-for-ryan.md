> **Voice rules (Elena, July 2026):** chill, human, zero sell. Open with the actual question ("we were wondering if X is interested in a pop up in NYC this fall"). One flat line on what we do that always covers all three: production, finding the spaces, and the buildouts. Elena's exact preferred phrasing: **"we find the space and do the buildout."** Always "buildout," never "construction." Never a self-intro, never "We're VenueDen." Close with a flat "let me know," or "let me know if you'd want to see spaces," never networking-speak like "if an intro would be useful" or "if that would be helpful." No benefit-selling, no urgency, no crafted hooks, no dashes, 3 to 4 short sentences, one or two word subjects, sign just "Ryan."
>
> **Neighborhoods:** VenueDen does all of NYC including Brooklyn (Williamsburg, SoHo, Nolita, Tribeca, and more). Naming a neighborhood is GOOD when it's strategic, it reads more personal than generic, so pick the one that fits the brand (their past pop up location, or the obvious home for their customer). Go generic ("NYC", "the city") only when there's no clear fit. Never imply we only do SoHo. No value-add asides either ("no long lease needed"), that's selling.
